## GregTech 5 Unofficial: granite textures

`tools/base/speckled_granite.png` and `tools/base/smooth_speckled_granite.png` are grey versions of GregTech's red
granite textures (`GRANITE_RED_STONE.png` and `GRANITE_RED_SMOOTH.png` from
https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/GT5-Unofficial, `src/main/resources/assets/gregtech/textures/blocks/iconsets/`),
the same files the Minecraft versions in Naverene/moredyes use. GT5-Unofficial is licensed under the GNU Lesser
General Public License, version 3 or later ([licenses/LGPL-3.0.txt](licenses/LGPL-3.0.txt), which builds on
[licenses/GPL-3.0.txt](licenses/GPL-3.0.txt)). These files, and the textures the generator makes from them
(`BlockTextures/MoreDyes_Granite.png`, `BlockTextures/MoreDyes_Granite_Smooth.png` and the `Granite_*` and
`Granite_Smooth_*` slab textures in `BlockTextures/MoreDyes/`), stay under that license.
